Music Trainer

Train your ear and your writing, right in the browser.

Four small apps for musicians and students: chords, counterpoint, dictation and harmony.

  • Free
  • No download or sign-up
  • Runs in your browser, phone or desktop
  • Works offline after the first visit

Chord Trainer

Hear and name chords beyond plain triads: 7ths, sus and add chords, extensions and altered dominants, with inversions. Puzzle, training and “imagine” modes, plus a progression sketchpad to loop ideas like Em9 | A13 | Cmaj7#11.

English · MIDI keyboard supported

Open Source on GitHub

Counterpointer

Species counterpoint, first and second species. Write against a cantus firmus (or one of Fux's) and see which rules each note breaks, with hints, fixes and a cheat sheet. Strict (Fux) or relaxed rules, in all church modes.

English and Greek · MIDI keyboard supported

Open Source on GitHub

Dicteeineer

Pitch and melodic dictation. Find single notes on a piano keyboard, with or without a reference, or write generated melodies bar by bar in any key, on treble, bass or grand staff, with optional chromatic notes.

English and Greek · MIDI keyboard supported

Open Source on GitHub

Harmoneer

Four-part harmony, first year: write soprano, alto and tenor over a figured bass, or harmonize a melody. Primary degrees, inversions, VI and II, cadences, III and VII, with the rules checked as you write, hints and a model solution.

English and Greek · MIDI keyboard supported

Open Source on GitHub

How it works

Open an app and start. Each one installs itself in the browser on the first visit (about 1 MB with the piano sounds), so it keeps working offline. You can also add it to your home screen.

Your progress and settings stay on your device. There are no accounts and nothing is sent anywhere.

A MIDI keyboard works in Chrome, Edge and Firefox. Safari doesn't support Web MIDI, so on an iPhone or iPad you answer on the screen.
